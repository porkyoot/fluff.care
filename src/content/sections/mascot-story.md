**Ullevi** is the heart and soul of **Care Fluff** — a round, huggable purple fox-tanuki with glowing amber-pink eyes, neon cyan hair and paw pads, and soft heart markings.

Her design blends our synthwave fox **Ullevi** with **Miyuki**, the work-in-progress pastel tanuki fursona of our beloved friend **Simone**. Simone was sweet, kind, and generous — very calm and patient, yet also wonderfully talkative. She was always together with her dog **Tuffsan**, and even took **Tuffsan** as her name online.

Simone was deeply loved and cherished, and we will always miss her. Through Ullevi's heart markings and warm spirit, a piece of Simone's kindness lives on in every corner of our world.
